A LETTER FOR YOU

HI ADI KOOO,

I don’t really know how to start this, but there’s something I’ve been wanting to tell you.

I miss you. I truly do. I miss talking to you, hearing from you, and simply having you in my life. I know that things between us didn’t work out, and maybe right now, we both need time and space to heal from everything that happened.

I’m not writing this to pressure you into coming back. I just want you to know what’s in my heart.

I still love you, and a part of me will always care about you. I’m willing to wait—not because I expect you to come back, but because what we had meant so much to me. If someday we find our way back to each other, I hope it happens when we’re both healed, ready, and able to love each other better. And if love ever finds me again, I honestly hope it’s with you.

But if someday you fall in love with someone else, I’ll accept that too. As much as I want you to be with me, I want you to be happy more than anything. If someone else can make you genuinely happy, then I’ll always wish you the best, even if it hurts me.

Please take care of yourself. I hope you heal from everything, find peace, and become happy again. I’ll always be grateful for the memories, the love, and the time we shared.

I really, love you. And even if we’re not talking anymore, I hope you know that you will always have a special place in my heart.

I miss you. I hope you’re okay. And most of all, I hope you’re happy.

Miss you Baho

From someone who thinks
you're pretty special.
